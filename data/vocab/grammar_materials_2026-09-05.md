# Prepositions

## Min | من
Core functions: origin, source, starting point, comparison, cause. English: from / out of / than.
- Ana Bashte8el min el-bait | I work from home.
- Ba7eb el-shai aktar min el-2ahweh | I like tea more than coffee.
- Ana ta3ban min el-shu8ul | I'm tired from work.

## 3ala | على
Core functions: location, destination, time/event. English: on / at / to.
- El-akel 3ala el-tawla | The food is on the table.
- Ana baru7 3ala el-bait | I go home.
- Ana bashrab 3asir 3ala el-3asha | I drink juice at dinner.

## Fi | في
Core functions: inside something physically or conceptually (place, time, condition); "there is". English: inside / in / there is.
- Ana bashte8el fi America | I work in America.
- Ana banam fi-ellail | I sleep at night.
- Fi mushkeleh fi hai el-8assaleh | There is a problem in this washing machine.

## Ma3 | مع
Core functions: accompaniment, togetherness. English: with.
- Ana baru7 3ala el-shu8ul ma3 sadiqi | أنا بروح على الشغل مع صاحبي | I go to work with my friend.
- Huwwe byakol El-akel ma3 3aseer | هو بياكل الأكل مع عصير | He eats the food with juice.
- Humme byeeju ma3i | هم بييجوا معي | They come with me.

## Bi | بِ
Core functions: location/inside, means, instrument/tool, engagement. English: by / with / in.
- Ana baaji bi-lseyyara | أنا باجي بالسيارة | I come by car.
- Huwwe ma bye7ki bi-ltelefon | هو ما بيحكي بالتلفون | He's not speaking on the phone.
- Humme byaklu bi-el-shoakeh u el-sekkineh | هم بياكلوا بالشوكة والسكينة | They eat with a fork and a knife.

## La | لَ
Core functions: possession, recipient, purpose, end point. English: for / to.
- Hada el-ktab la-mehdi | هاد الكتاب لمهدي | This book is for Mehdi.
- Huwwe beynam min el-lail la-lsubu7 | هو بينام من الليل للصبح | He sleeps from night to morning.
- Ana bat3allam 3arabi la2dar a7ki ma3kom | أنا بتعلم عربي لأقدر أحكي معكم | I learn Arabic to be able to speak with you.
- Enti laazem te7kilo enno huwwe naaje7 | أنتِ لازم تحكيلو انو هو ناجح | You should tell him that he's successful.
- Hadi el moazeh eli mish elo | هادي الموزة الي مش الو | This banana is mine not his.

# Noun possessives
In spoken Arabic, possession is shown by putting two nouns next to each other. No apostrophes, no "of": main noun, then the owner.

## Proper nouns pattern
Thing + name. No el- on the first noun; proper nouns make it definite.
- Mehdi's book = Ktaab Mehdi. California Street = Share3 California. John's shirt = Bloozet John.
If the first noun is feminine, add "t" to its end before linking.

## Nouns with pronouns pattern
Thing + pronoun-ended noun. No el- on either noun.
- My hand's finger = Usba3 eedi. My friend's room = 8urfet sadiqi. My family's house = Bait 3ailti.

## Common / regular nouns pattern
First noun loses el-; the second noun decides definiteness.
- Work clothes = Lebes el-shu8ul. Home food = Akel el-bait. Kitchen's table = Tawlet el-matba5.

## Chain possession pattern
Thing → owner → owner …; only the last noun decides definiteness.
- My friend's sister's book = Ktab u5t sadiqi. The car's work chair = Kursi seyyaret el-shu8ul. The family's bedroom's light = Dawu 8urfet noam el-3aile.

## Describing professions
Profession first, then the specification: Doktoar snaan (dentist), M3allem 3arabi (Arabic teacher), Ustaaz Farsi (Farsi teacher).
With "the", el- goes on the specification: Doktoar el-3yoon, M3allem el3arabi, Ustaaz el-englizi.
A pronoun on the first noun already makes it specific, no el-: Doctoarhom elsnaan, M3almi el3arabi, Ustaazo elfarsi.

# Noun + adjective definition
Adjectives go after the noun: Bait kbeer (big house), Seyyara 2adeema (old car), 8urfe ndeefa (clean room).
Known noun: el- on the noun, not on the adjective (a statement): El-bet 7elwa (the girl is beautiful), Hadi el-blooze kum, 3ailetna kbeera. A noun ending in a pronoun is definite by default.
To define the noun (a phrase, not a statement): el- on noun AND adjective: el-lebes el-jdeed (the new clothes), el-kursi el-3aali, el-fus6aan el8aali.

# Time and clock
Waqt = the actual time; Saa3a = clock / hour / watch; Marra = turn.
"The time is …" = El-saa3a + feminine number: wa7de (1), tentain (2), talaate (3) …
Minutes: add u/w + number: El-saa3a talaate u 3ashara (3:10), Elsaa3a wa7de u 5amse (1:05).
Rube3 = quarter past; Noss = half past; Ella rube3 = quarter to.
Singular / dual / plural: d2ee2a, D2ee2tain, d2aaye2 (minutes); saanie, Saaneitain, Sawaani (seconds); Saa3a, Saa3tain, Sa3aat (hours). 3:02 = El-saa3a talaate u d2ee2tain.
"At" a time = 3ala: 3ala elsaa3a 3ashara (at 10:00).
What time is it? = Qaddaish el-saa3a / Shu el saa3a. What time is dinner? = Ay saa3a el-3asha?

# Emotions / adjectives
Adjectives usually take "min": Ana 5aayef minnak/ek (scared of you), Heyye mitdaay2a minno (upset with him), Humme ma7roojeen minna (embarrassed of us).
Others: 2al2aan (worried) + 3ala; Mishtaa2 (missing) + la; Mu5talef / 8eir (different) + 3an.

## Plural forms
Masculine/general plural: + "een" (Humme mabsoo6een). Feminine plural: + "aat" (Humme 7anoonaat).

## Use of "Bakoon"
Bakoon = I am usually / I tend to be / I will be / I would be. It adds habit, future or conditional meaning.
- Normal present: no "to be", no bakoon: Ana ta3baan (I'm tired), Huwwe jaahez (he's ready).
- Habitual: Bakoon + adjective: Ana bakoon kasool ba3ed el-shu8ul; Marrat heyye betkoon ju3aane billail; E7na benkoon mash8uleen yoam el-etnain.
- Future: Bukra beykoon mabsoo6; Ra7 akoon mitwatter el-usboo3 el-jaay.
- Conditional: Ana bakoon mitdaay2a iza ma btiji ma3i; Heyye betkoon mad8oo6a lamma ykoon 3endha shu8ul kteer.
"when" (lamma) and "if" (iza) signal a conditional sentence → use bakoon / ykoon.

# Use of Kul
All = kul + el- (kul el-yoam, kul el-naas, kul el-usboo3). Every / each = kul without el- (kul yoam, kul 7ada, kul usboo3).

# Verb "b" prefix elimination
The b- prefix (ba7ki, bitshoof, biroou7) marks the present indicative: real, habitual, happening now.
Drop the b- when the action is uncertain, desired, or dependent on another word:
- Intent / necessity: Biddi ashrab; Lazim tertaa7; Mumkin asa3dak?
- Time conjunctions: Abel-ma yiju; Ba3ed-ma teshra7i; Lamma a6la3; Humme ra7 yjibu el-3asha ma3hom.
- Purpose: La- (Beddi aru7 3ala el-dukkan la-ashtri khobez); 3ashaan (Ana beddi ashufo 3ashan a7kilo eshi).
- Compound verbs: first verb carries tense, second has no b-: Ana baballesh amshi …; Huwwe beyjarreb ye3mal ryaada aktar; E7na ma bne2dar n5alli el-baab maftoo7 bellail.
- Chain reaction: after a gatekeeper (Biddi, Ba7eb, ra7, Laazim) everything joined by u / wala / aw stays without b-: Ana ba7eb a66alla3 3ala el-njoom u a7ki ma3ha; Ma ba3ref a7ki 3arabi wala aktubo; Beddak tiji 3alinna aw e7na niji 3alaik.
Keep the b- after "enno" (that): it starts a new real sentence: Ana bazonn enno ma bey7ebna; Ba3raf enno bteshte8li hoan.

# Pointer rule
If the object comes before the verb, the verb needs a pointer pronoun (-o him/it, -ha her/it) linking back.
- Superlatives (Aktar, A7san, Aswa2, A2al): Aktar eshi basawwih; A7san wa7de ba3rafha; Shu aswa2 tamrin bte3malo; Hadi a2al akleh bnetbo5ha; Min a7san zboon bteshte8el ma3o.
- Ay / Kul move the object to the front: Ay 7ada bashufo; Kul kursi byishteruh; Ay makaan benru7 3alaih; Kul sadiq bnelte2i ma3o; Ay mat3am betfadlu taaklu fih.
- Introducing indefinite nouns: Fi zalame ma ba3rafo barra; 3endo tilfizion ma byista5demo; Lamma tlaa2u eshi bet7ebbuh; Beddhom felem ye7daruh; Laazem tlaa2u eshi te3maluh.
