# Anees on the phone: 5 ways to replace the wrapped top menu (2026-10-06)

The problem (your screenshot): on a phone the 10 menu links wrap into 4 rows and push the page a full screen down.

What the research says (read 2026-10-06):
- Nielsen Norman Group, "Hamburger Menus and Hidden Navigation Hurt UX Metrics": hidden menus are used less and tasks take longer; a hamburger fits many links but "out of sight is out of mind".
- Nielsen Norman Group, "Basic Patterns for Mobile Navigation": tab bars show 3-5 destinations and are the default for apps you USE (not browse); menus suit browse-mostly content.
- Comparison study (DiVA, 2016): a bottom bar beats a hamburger on task completion time.
- Apple Human Interface Guidelines (tab bars) and Google Material 3 (navigation bar): 3-5 destinations, labels on every tab, the bar at the bottom where the thumb is.

Today's menu has 10 items. The research answer is: show the 3-5 you use daily, hide the rest behind one "More".

| # | Option | How it looks on the phone | Good | Bad |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bottom tab bar + More (recommended) | 5 tabs at the bottom: Progress, Lessons, Cards, Student, More. More = Word Bank, Grammar, Tutor, AI Reports, Big Picture, Settings | the standard every app uses; thumb reach; page starts at the top | 5 of 10 are one tap further |
| 2 | Two bars: role switch + tabs | a small Student / Teacher switch at the top; bottom tabs change with the role (Student: Progress, Lessons, Cards, Homework · Teacher: To do, Grammar, Vocab, Decay) | the Tutor and Student worlds stop sharing one menu | two things to learn; Amal only ever uses Teacher |
| 3 | Scrolling chip row | one horizontal row of chips under the logo, swipe to see the rest, active one first | one row, no hidden menu, no bottom bar | people miss what is off-screen; still 10 items |
| 4 | Hamburger (Menu) + page title | logo + "Menu" word top-left, the whole list in a drawer | smallest header; nothing to decide | the pattern research scores worst for use; one more tap every time |
| 5 | Home hub + back | the phone opens on a 10-tile home screen; every page has only "‹ Home" | zero header on every page; tiles can show counts | two taps between any two pages; Progress is no longer the home |

Pick 1 unless you want the Student / Teacher split (2). Laptop stays as it is (left sidebar).
