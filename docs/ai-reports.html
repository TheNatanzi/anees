D

Update his records

same run
  1. 9

    Word Bank: score every word he said

    Each Arabic word is matched to the tutor's vocabulary Doc. A missed word turns Shaky; 2 right in a row with no help brings it back. Speaking and flashcards keep separate scores. A wrong sound or a grammar slip never counts as a missed word.

    Automatic
  2. 10

    Grammar: count every use, not only the mistakes

    Each time he uses a rule (the date, the clock, a b-verb) it counts, so a rule he always gets right still shows up. Mistakes count only in rules the tutor approved. Under 30 sentences, the % shows grey as early. Her rules page shows the same numbers.

    AutomaticThe tutor
  3. 11

    Progress & Stats: summaries only

    Words %, grammar %, fluency, homework and lesson count, all counted from the same marked transcript. A check fails if any page is out of date.

    Automatic
    StudyListening research: what predicts what he understands (Sep 27)

    Best predictor: does the line hold a word he doesn't know yet. Result: the list of listening tags worth tracking, unknown words first. Read the study ›

  4. 12

    Missed words go into Flashcards

    The Shaky words set holds every word he got wrong or asked for in the last 2 lessons. Lists the tutor uploads become their own sets. Tapping Attention on a card sends his question to the tutor.

    AutomaticThe student
  5. 13

    The Tutor page gets her to-do list

    Words she taught that are not in her Doc go on her New words card (she picks NEW or OLD). Held lines, unsure mistakes, grammar to approve and his flashcard questions land there too.

    AutomaticThe tutor
