How the memory estimate works

Starting bands: Strong under 14 days; Fading 14–20; At risk 21–34; High risk 35+.

Only full-credit recall restarts the clock, including self-correction before help. Hints, misses, hearing Amal and repeating her do not restart it.

Two successful recalls across longer gaps can extend a word’s window, up to twice the starting bands. A miss removes that extension and shows at least At risk until full-credit recall. A hinted answer shows at least Fading.

This is a conservative starting heuristic, not a measured recall probability. Individual word dates and decay details are available in the Word Bank. Speaking and flashcards remain separate.
