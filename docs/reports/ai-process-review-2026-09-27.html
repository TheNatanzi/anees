Next: measuring the sentence-ladder labeller

With 10 swipes per lesson, the error bar is wide early on. It narrows only if every swipe is logged against a fixed labeller version.

new datainspo · active-learning sampling
  • Sample on purpose: 4 of the 10 swipes go to the labeller's least-sure sentences, 5 are random, and 1 repeats an old swipe to check your own consistency.
  • Log label_version · prompt_sha · label · confidence · your_swipe for each item. Keep a confusion matrix for understood, breakdown and unknown.
  • Freeze the labeller for a week. Change it only after scoring the new version on all past swipes, never on new ones alone.
  • Gate: if you reject more than 20% of labels in 2 lessons in a row, retune. Amal checks 20 labels a month to calibrate you.
About 12 lessons (120 swipes) to get within ±7 points.95% interval at 80% accuracy
