    How progress works
    • Speaking New: reach 5 uses by you, even in one lesson. Helped uses count as practice—not proof of mastery.
    • Flashcards New: 5 consecutive first-try successes across 2+ dates.
    • Recovery: Missed → success → Shaky → next success → Good. Speaking uses separate lesson dates; a miss takes precedence within its date.
    • Speaking Mastered: 5 consecutive successful lesson dates (one credit per word/date). Flashcards Mastered: 5 consecutive first-try successes across 3+ dates.
    • Speaking is transcript-detected, not audio-verified. Amal’s words and typed homework do not count.
