    How progress works
    • Speaking New: reach 5 uses by you, even in one lesson. Helped uses count as practice—not proof of mastery.
    • Listening counts a word whenever it appears in Amal’s timed recorded speech. It tracks exposure, not whether you understood it.
    • Flashcards New: 5 consecutive first-try successes across 2+ dates.
    • In each scored lane: Missed → 1 independent success → Shaky → 1 more → Good.
    • Mastered: 5 consecutive successes in that score across 3+ dates. Speaking earns at most one success per word/lesson date.
    • Speaking is transcript-detected, not audio-verified. Amal’s words never count as your Speaking uses.
