What each number means

  • Practice occurrences: identified words or phrases actually spoken by Medi. Genuine repetitions count. A wrong contextual choice may still be an occurrence of the word actually said.
  • Assessment classes: independent, helped, recall failure, incorrect and unresolved are mutually exclusive for each evidence item.
  • Counts overlap: recorded uses overlap independent, helped, spoken incorrect and spoken unresolved evidence. Recall failure with no target spoken adds a miss but no use. Do not add the columns to derive total uses.
  • Unresolved: includes ambiguous matching, uncertain wording and uncertain assessment. Some unresolved items have a uniquely identified spoken target; others receive no count.
  • Provisional / human reviewed: describe the assessment's provenance, not extra occurrences. Amal-reviewed wording alone is not Amal-approved correctness.
  • Mastery credits: at most one per word per lesson date, after daily miss precedence. Total independent occurrences may be larger. The current consecutive-success streak is distinct from lifetime credits and total practice dates.

Matching and evidence rules

  • Use 2,120 active vocabulary entries and their stored approved aliases. The available Google Doc snapshot is dated September 4, exported September 10. A live published-Doc URL is not configured; this is not claimed fresh.
  • Arabic normalization preserves candidate collisions. بضحك / بضحِّك, بزهق / بزهِّق and خيار senses remain unresolved unless context explicitly resolves the candidate. يوم and اليوم remain distinct.
  • Exact Arabizi and approved aliases only. English stopwords and mixed-script boundaries block false matches; English is never deleted to join a phrase.
  • Within a source row, the longest matching phrase owns its span. Components receive no extra credit. No phrase crosses a speaker turn or a gap over 1.2 seconds. A collision remains unresolved rather than falling back to convenient components.
  • First-person أنا بكسر may match bare بكسر. A finite audited break paradigm maps transitive conjugations to that entry. Ambiguous بنكسر, intransitive انكسر and attempted بخسر are not silently credited as break. Other unlisted conjugations may remain unmatched.
  • Stable identities bind lesson, recording hash and local time span. Source overlap checks prevent double credit. Original item IDs, wording, source hash, local and meeting times, match method, context and later corrections remain attached.
  • Tutor, unknown/background, typed homework, flashcard answers and excluded language never count. Reading homework aloud is recorded helped practice; typed homework is excluded.
