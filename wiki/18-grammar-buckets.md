# 18 — Grammar buckets (Levantine / Palestinian, Amal's shape)

**What this is.** Every grammar rule Medi needs, cut into named buckets he can be scored on, one bucket per rule.
Built from Amal's two Docs (vocabulary Doc + "Arabic Materials" grammar Doc, snapshot 2026-09-05), the Aug 25 / Sep 4 / Sep 14–21 transcripts, and the ten published Levantine sequences already sourced in [17 — Teacher brain](17-teacher-brain-levantine-arabic.md) sections B and C (KCL, OSU/Abuhakema, Wikibooks, mossyrune, Routledge Al-Masri, Younes, SOAS, BU, Rice & Sa'id *Eastern Arabic*, Hitchcock & Shakkour *A Learner's Grammar of Levantine Arabic*, plus Cowell as the reference grammar).

**Columns.**
- *Amal* — does her own material teach it? (Doc = in her Docs, lesson = taught in a lesson, gap = neither)
- *Medi* — has he slipped on it in a recorded lesson? (rank = its place in the hardest-ten list in 17-C)
- *Order* — the teaching order the ten curricula share (17-B), not Amal's order

Ordering note: Amal front-loads the verb system and teaches the small grammar as conversation throws it up. These buckets keep her rules but are grouped by family so a score can be read per family.

## Family A — The noun phrase (the "el" family)

| # | Bucket | The rule in one line | Amal | Medi | Order |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
