How to review · read this first

Listen before judging. Review A and B independently. A/B ordering is fixed for this set; source names are hidden until you choose to reveal them.

Choose Exact if the transcript matches what you hear. Choose Minor or Wrong and type what you actually hear if it needs a correction. Choose Can’t tell if you cannot determine the wording. Keep spoken mistakes, repetitions, fillers and incomplete words; do not substitute the intended correct word.

There is no second confirmation step: clicking Exact confirms the original wording; entering a correction records the words you heard. An unfinished correction, [unclear], or Can’t tell stays unverified. Use Add missing speech if needed, then Next clip. You may skip anything you cannot review; technical checks and notes are optional.

The A/B comparison is calculated from your saved wording when you download your review. No overall vote is needed. It compares word edits only where the same audio has a shared, fully reviewed written reference; incomplete or conflicting evidence stays pending. Results are not shown while you review, so they cannot influence the next answer.

Named speakers indicate the source track, not an acoustic identity check. Mixed-recording speaker labels remain uncertain. You can audition the mixed audio and each named track.

Twenty 25-second windows already contain over eight minutes of audio. A careful first review can take longer than five minutes. Review a subset, download your progress, and resume later. The eventual 3–5 minute goal needs measurement.

No human gold is assumed here. Downloads are human-review records; only explicitly confirmed wording is eligible for a reference set after conflicts are resolved.
