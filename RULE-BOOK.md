# Anees rule book

Every rule Anees follows, in plain words, in the order a lesson happens.

**What the badges mean**

- **Automatic**: the computer checks it every time.
- **Written down**: written where the AI helpers read it; no automatic check yet.
- **Fixed once**: only one moment was fixed so far.
- **Waiting on you**: needs your decision.

**Words used here**

- **Doc**: Amal's vocabulary Google Doc: the master list of words.
- **Arabizi**: Arabic written in Latin letters and numbers, the way Amal types it.
- **Slip**: a grammar mistake that counts.
- **Rule group**: a named grammar pattern, like B8 (also called a bucket).
- **AI readers**: the AI that reads each lesson transcript and finds the moments.
- **Publish check**: the test that must pass before anything goes on the live site.

## The big rules

- **S1 · Amal's spelling is the only spelling.** Every Arabic word is spelled the way Amal spells it: first how she types it, then her Doc's Arabizi, then her Arabic script; never a made-up spelling. `AZ-01`
- **S2 · Raw transcripts are never edited.** The transcript the machine writes is never changed; fixes are kept in a separate layer on top. `TR-01`
- **S3 · A slip is only a slip when there is a signal.** A grammar slip counts only when there is a signal: Amal corrects or re-says it, or you ask about it. `GR-01`
- **S4 · Pronunciation is never a missed word.** Saying a word with a wrong sound (like a dropped ع or ط) is a pronunciation note: it never counts as a missed word and never lowers the word's score. `WS-01`
- **S5 · Pauses are not errors.** Pauses are not mistakes: a long pause and then the right answer is a right answer. `GR-02`
- **S6 · Every correction becomes a rule.** Every correction becomes a rule: fix that one moment, and write the general rule where the next lesson will meet it, listed in the rule list. `PR-01`
- **S7 · A page is never older than its source.** A page is never older than its data: when a newer lesson exists the page says so, and the publish check warns if a lesson is missing from the site, Amal's answers were not checked for 2 hours, or publishing has been stuck for 6 hours. `PG-15`
